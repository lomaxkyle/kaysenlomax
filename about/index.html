Project · Marketing

Southern Pharmacy

Role
Marketing Coordinator
When
Aug 2024 – May 2025, with pieces through 2026
Scope
Social media · print · events · campaign pitch · website concept
Where
Jonesboro, Arkansas

Southern Pharmacy is a hometown pharmacy with stores across Northeast Arkansas. For ten months I was its Marketing Coordinator: I ran the social accounts, photographed products and events, and designed everything that went out in-store and online. The work kept coming after the job ended.

The biggest piece was a full campaign pitch under the line “Big Time Service. Small Town Care.” It ran 43 slides: audience and tone, 40 scheduled social posts, 10 radio spots, five video concepts, a website concept and a four-phase rollout plan.

Around it: a weekly Wellness Tip Wednesday series, launches for MPowder and CarePacks, the invitation for the Hormone House Party, a menopause awareness event for 50 invited women from across the region, a tornado response post the night two towns were hit, and in December 2025 the announcement that two locations were passing to another pharmacy.

My photography business needed a mark that could sit on a business card, a flyer, an Instagram post and a phone screen without changing its character. I built it as a system: one stacked wordmark in three colorways, a five-color palette and a set of pieces that all use the same type and spacing.

About

Nice to meet you

I’m Kaysen Lomax, a photographer and multimedia creator in Jonesboro, Arkansas. I’ve photographed seniors, couples, families and events across Northeast Arkansas since 2018, and since January 2026 I’ve shot weddings as a contract photographer with Rustic Pine Media.

I also shoot and edit video. On the design side I make graphics for print and social media, from invitations to ads. I spent the 2024–25 school year as Marketing Coordinator for Southern Pharmacy, running its social media, photographing its events and designing the graphics that went with them. My work placed first in Portrait Photography and second in Sports Photography at the 2023 Great A-State Media Fest.

I’m a member of Chi Omega at A-State. I served a term as our Panhellenic Delegate, I’m now our Personnel Chair, and since 2024 I’ve made the chapter’s recruitment films.

I graduate from Arkansas State University in December 2026 with a B.S. in Mass Communications in Creative Media Production.
